**文案**：公主赵鸾登场，凤冠威仪，面无表情

**视频提示词**：
```
运镜：Low angle tracking shot, camera starts at princess's golden phoenix crown close-up, slowly push out to reveal full regal entrance, measured footsteps echoing in silent hall, 4-second hold on static frame after stops

空间：Princess enters from left door, walks 8 steps to center frame, two armored guards in V-formation 3 steps behind, imperial seal medallion visible on belt, symmetrical composition

主体：Princess wearing elaborate nine-phoenix golden crown (九翟凤冠), red and gold imperial robes (翟衣), hands clasped in front, face completely emotionless, cold piercing eyes staring straight ahead, measured confident stride

灯光：Single dramatic key light from above creating volumetric god rays, deep shadows carving facial features, golden rim light tracing crown edges, warm amber fill on robe shadows

色调：Deep amber and gold tones, slight film desaturation, anamorphic lens flare on crown

氛围：Ominous power, absolute authority, tension building, silence before violence

一致性：Lock to [PRINCESS_REF_01], maintain crown position at all times
```
